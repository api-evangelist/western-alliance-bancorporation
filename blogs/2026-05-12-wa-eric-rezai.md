Mr. Rezai brings more than 15 years of experience in healthcare and commercial banking. Prior to joining Western Alliance Bank, he held relationship management and leadership roles at City National Bank, where he served as vice president and relationship manager on the Corporate Banking Healthcare team with primary coverage of the Western and Midwest regions, and at Truist Bank where he supported healthcare and commercial clients as vice president in Commercial Banking. Earlier in his career, he spent several years at Fifth Third Bank, completing a leadership development program with experience spanning relationship management, strategy, finance and accounting and servicing as a relationship manager in both the Ohio and Florida markets. Mr. Rezai earned a Bachelor of Arts in Finance and Marketing from Xavier University. He is based in Southern California and works with healthcare clients nationwide, with a primary focus on clients in the Western United States.
